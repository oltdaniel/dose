# Homepage

## Surface

Read: a personal site's homepage and article-reading pages.

The author needs a place to publish articles and introduce himself briefly.
Readers need to find an article, read it comfortably, and reach contact or
social links when those have been configured. The author and user asked for a
warm off-colour white, typewriter-style typography, simple shapes and lines,
and no unnecessary complexity. Email and social destinations remain open
because none were supplied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a small, quiet index of writing, not a showcase or a theme
demo. It refuses cards, decorative separators, theme controls, and unnecessary
metadata.

OWN-WORLD: Courier Prime on a warm off-white ground; near-black type, muted
secondary text, thin neutral rules, underlined links, and one small square
marker per article. The reading column stays narrow and plain.

STORY: The visitor sees the author's name and the writing list, opens an
article, then finds a concise author note and any configured contact or social
links at the bottom. The author's role is explicitly undefined.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A centered, single-column page with the name at the top,
followed by a "Writing" heading and a direct list of article titles and dates.
Each row is a full link with a small outlined square and a thin bottom rule.
There is no hero image, card grid, or secondary navigation.

FORM: Brief-pinned typewritten index, position 1/1; code-led because no image
generation or decision-page tool is available. The author's explicit
direction takes precedence over the seeded alternatives. seed:
