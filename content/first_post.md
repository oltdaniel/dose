My name is Greg. I am from Cape Town, South Africa, and I am a passionate technology and electronics enthusiast with a deep love for design and the arts. My interests are broad and varied, and I am a committed, self-directed learner who enjoys exploring ideas, skills, and disciplines with curiosity and purpose.

I have developed an internal learning practice that I call the perpetual beginner: a mindset of continually studying the subjects, methods, and skills that matter most to me, starting from the fundamentals in order to understand not just the what and the how, but also the why, the context, and the deeper purpose behind them. This approach has helped me build a strong foundation in each area I explore, allowing me to grow from solid understanding to more advanced capability with confidence and clarity.

That approach is also how I have returned to programming. I once thought of myself as a coder because I knew the terminology and some of JavaScript's higher-level features, but eventually realised I needed to build a much stronger foundation. After a few years focused elsewhere, I am now committed to developing my skills and growing into a capable developer over time.

As a result, I see myself as a broad generalist by design — not by limitation, but by choice. With the support of a personalised AI assistant setup, I can selectively gather the right resources and references to build a focused, deeper understanding within a specific domain while keeping the wider context in view. This allows me to design a learning path that connects theory, methodology, and practical implementation in a meaningful way.

I am not an expert in everything, and I do not claim mastery in any single field. But I have come to value the strength of being endlessly curious, adaptable, and willing to learn from the ground up. Life has presented significant challenges, including long-term struggles with mental health, yet those experiences have shaped my resilience and deepened my commitment to growth, self-development, and meaningful work.

Today, I work as a contractor and run my own small business, bringing together the skills I have accumulated into useful, marketable services. I am building a stable career and a sustainable income while keeping life intentionally simple, focused, and aligned with my values. I prioritise personal growth, meaningful experiences, and financial literacy over excess, and I approach money with care and purpose as I work toward long-term stability and independence.

This blog is one of my most serious and meaningful efforts yet: a consistent, thoughtful practice of creating, learning, and sharing work in public. It is a space for reflection, exploration, and gradual progress — a record of my journey toward deeper understanding, better craft, and a more intentional life.
